# Dubbodu Privacy Policy

## Overview
Dubbodu is an independent campus companion application developed by Team Navishka for students and campus residents.

This repository hosts the static privacy policy page (`index.html`) published for the application.

## Policy Principles & Architecture
- **Zero Personal User Data**: The app does not collect personal identity information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, or private communications.
- **On-Device Permissions**: GPS location and orientation/gyroscope motion sensor capabilities are processed in real-time on the device and never transmitted or saved remotely.
- **Device & Account Identification**: The app securely processes necessary technical device and account identification IDs strictly for session management, app integrity, and abuse prevention. These technical identifiers are never linked to real-world personal identities or user behavior.
- **No Commercial Tracking or Profiling**: No third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or data brokers are integrated.
- **CDN Asset Delivery**: Campus map data and panoramic images are delivered via Cloudflare CDN over HTTPS.

## Recent Updates
- **Data Collection Clarification**: Updated `index.html` to clearly and positively communicate the handling of device and account identification IDs for technical security and seamless operation, while firmly emphasizing the strict zero-personal-user-data guarantee.
