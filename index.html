Dubbodu Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: Dubbodu (com.nandu45321.dubbodu)

Dubbodu is a campus panorama viewer and companion app for IISER Thiruvananthapuram, developed by Your Full Name ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Information we use

  • Location (GPS). If you allow location access, the app uses your device's location to show where you are on the campus map and to help with navigation. VERIFY: Location is processed on your device only and is not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app shows alerts and reminders on your device. It may run a foreground service so these keep working while the app is in the background.
  • Content you share. When you use the share feature, the app hands the content you choose to the app you select (for example a messaging app). We do not receive it.
  • Network requests. The app downloads panoramas and campus data from our servers (hosted on Cloudflare). Like any server, these may log technical details such as your IP address, request time and app version for operation and abuse prevention. VERIFY: No account, name, email or other personal profile is collected.

2. How we use information

Only to provide and improve the features above: showing your position, delivering notifications, loading app content, keeping the service secure and fixing bugs.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising and the app contains no ads. We share information only with service providers needed to run the app (such as Cloudflare for hosting and delivery), or if the law requires it.

4. Data retention

VERIFY: We do not keep personal location data. Server logs, if any, are kept for a limited period (for example up to 30 days) and then deleted.

5. Your choices

  • You can turn off location or notification permission at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Permissions. Some features will stop working.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes locally stored app data from your device.
  • To ask a question or request deletion of anything we may hold about you, contact us (see below).

6. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the information we handle.

7. Children

Dubbodu is intended for a general campus audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be at this page, with its effective date shown at the top.
