Dubbodu — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Developer: Team Navishka

Unofficial app. Dubbodu is an independent, student-led project by Team Navishka. It is not an official app of IISER Thiruvananthapuram and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the institute.
Accuracy disclaimer. Information displayed in the app (timetables, mess menus, bus timings, academic calendar, contacts, etc.) is aggregated on a best-effort basis and may not always be accurate or up to date. Use it at your own risk. Always verify with official institute sources for anything important.

What we collect

  • Location (GPS). Only used to show your position on the campus map and compute routes. Processed on your device — never sent to our servers.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer). Only used to control the 360° panorama viewer. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Timetable preferences. Your course, year, and subject selections are saved locally on your device only. Never uploaded.
  • Device identifiers (planned). To prevent abuse, the app plans to collect an anonymous device identifier (Android ID + a random install ID) on first launch to register your installation. This is not linked to your name, email, or any personal account. You will be informed at first launch.
  • Server request logs. When the app fetches content (panoramas, menus, timings, etc.), our servers may log your IP address, request time, and app version for technical operation. These are not linked to any personal profile.

What we do with it

  • Power the map, routing, and 360° viewer
  • Show your timetable and automate silent mode during class hours
  • Serve campus information (menus, timings, calendar, contacts)
  • Prevent abuse of the service
  • Fix bugs and keep the app running

What we never do

  • Sell your data
  • Show ads or use ad networks
  • Track your location over time or build a profile on you
  • Require an account or store personal information

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — hosts and delivers app content (panoramas, data). May process your IP address as part of delivery.
  • Firebase (Google) — planned for anonymous device registration (abuse prevention only). No personal data involved.

Security

All network requests use HTTPS. The app's assets are encrypted. Cached panoramas on your device are stored encrypted and are only usable on your device. We apply reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

  • Location: Revoke in Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Permissions. Map and routing stop working; everything else still works.
  • Notifications: Revoke in Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Notifications.
  • Uninstall: Removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Data requests: Contact us below.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest change.
