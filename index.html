Dubbodu — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · App: Dubbodu · Developer: Team Navishka / Yamulapalli Nandu Navadeep, IISER Thiruvananthapuram

Dubbodu is a campus companion app for IISER Thiruvananthapuram. It provides 360° panorama navigation, GPS-assisted campus routing, timetable management, mess menu, bus timings, academic calendar, contact directory, and related campus information services. This policy explains exactly what data the app uses, why, and what we never do.

Short version: The app does not create accounts, does not track you, does not sell data, and does not show ads. Nearly everything stays on your own device.

1. Information the app uses

a. Location (GPS and device sensors)

  • GPS / fine location — used to show your position on the campus map and to calculate walking routes. Location is processed entirely on-device. It is never sent to, stored on, or logged by our servers.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, gravity, magnetometer) — used locally to control the 360° panorama viewer (tilting your phone changes the view direction). No sensor data leaves your device.

b. Timetable preferences

If you set up your timetable (course, year, subject selections), those preferences are stored locally on your device in a configuration file. They are never uploaded to our servers. The app uses them to show your schedule and — if you enable silent mode — to mute your device automatically during class hours.

c. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app may show on-device alerts (for example, class reminders or campus updates). The app may run a foreground service to keep these working in the background. No notification content is sent to us.

d. Content you share

If you use the share feature (for example, to share a bus timing or contact), the app passes the content you select to the app you choose (a messaging or email app). We do not receive a copy.

e. Network requests and server logs

The app downloads campus data (panoramas, timetable data, mess menus, bus timings, contacts, academic calendar) from our servers. Like any server, these may record standard technical details — your device's IP address, request timestamp, requested resource, and app version — solely for operation, debugging, and abuse prevention. We do not create user profiles. No name, email, account, or device identifier is linked to these logs.

2. Panoramic image privacy

All 360° panoramas published through the app are subject to the following safeguards:

  • Automatic face blurring. Every panoramic image is run through a computer-vision pipeline (OpenCV) that detects and blurs all human faces before the image is uploaded to the server. No identifiable face is published.
  • Full person removal (planned). For cases where individuals appear prominently, a two-photograph compositing technique will be used to remove them entirely from the final image.
  • Restricted photography scope. Photography is limited to outdoor areas and common indoor areas (corridors, staircases, entrance lobbies, publicly accessible common rooms). Laboratories, faculty offices, restricted zones, security infrastructure, and any space flagged as sensitive by the institute are explicitly excluded. Any indoor space beyond common areas requires explicit written permission from the responsible authority.
  • No user-generated content. Users cannot upload images, post comments, or contribute any content. All published material is curated entirely by the developer and subject to institute oversight.
  • Remote removal. Any panorama can be removed from the app remotely within minutes of a request — by institute authorities or by us — without requiring an app update.

3. Indoor maps

Indoor navigation uses simplified, node-based vector maps derived from publicly displayed floor plans. No detailed architectural drawings, structural layouts, or engineering schematics are published. Room-level content requires separate permission from the responsible authority.

4. How we use information

Solely to provide and improve the features described above:

  • Show your position on the campus map
  • Calculate and display walking routes
  • Drive the 360° panorama viewer
  • Display your timetable and automate silent mode
  • Deliver campus information (mess menus, bus timings, contacts, academic calendar)
  • Keep the service secure and fix bugs

5. What we never do

We never…Why this is guaranteed
Sell your personal data No personal data is collected in the first place
Show ads or use ad networks The app has no advertising and no monetization through user data
Track your location over time GPS is only read when the map or routing feature is active; nothing is stored or uploaded
Create user accounts or profiles There is no login, sign-up, or user identity system
Use cross-app or cross-site tracking No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting

6. Data sharing

We share information only in the following narrow circumstances:

  • Infrastructure providers. Campus data and panoramas are hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure (a globally trusted CDN and security platform). Cloudflare may process technical metadata (IP address, request details) as part of delivering content. Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • Legal obligations. If required by applicable law or a lawful governmental order.

No other third party receives your data.

7. Data retention

  • We do not retain personal location data.
  • Server logs, if any, are kept for a limited period (up to 30 days) and then deleted.
  • Your timetable preferences and locally cached campus data live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Future features

Dubbodu is an evolving platform. Planned features and their privacy implications are disclosed here in advance:

  • Student verification. If the institute requires that certain content be accessible only to enrolled students, a lightweight verification step (for example, using an institute email address) may be introduced. Any such system will be described in an updated policy before it is deployed. No academic record or personal data beyond email verification would be stored.
  • Push notifications. Institution-wide announcements or personalised schedule reminders may be delivered via a push notification service. Device tokens used for delivery would not be linked to personal identity.
  • Events and community features. If community or event features are added, any content you submit will be covered by updated terms communicated clearly at the time.
  • Expanded indoor maps and navigation. Additional buildings may be mapped with institute permission. The same restrictions (common areas only, no sensitive spaces, no structural data) will continue to apply.

This policy will be updated before any new data practice takes effect.

9. Institute oversight

IISER Thiruvananthapuram authorities retain the ability to request removal or modification of any content in the app at any time. A dedicated point of contact is available to respond promptly. The system is designed so that content can be updated or removed server-side without requiring users to update the app.

10. Security

  • All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Panoramas are served from Cloudflare and are not publicly indexable by search engines.
  • No system is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest.

11. Children

Dubbodu is intended for the IISER TVM campus community (students, faculty, staff, and visitors). It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Your choices

  • Location: Revoke at any time in Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Permissions. Map and routing features will stop working; all other features remain available.
  • Notifications: Revoke at any time in Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Notifications.
  • Timetable data: You can clear or reset your timetable preferences within the app at any time.
  • Uninstall: Removes all locally stored app data from your device.
  • Data requests: To ask about, or request deletion of, anything we may hold, contact us at the address below.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features evolve. The latest version will always be at this URL, with the effective date shown at the top. For material changes, we will make a reasonable effort to notify users in-app.
