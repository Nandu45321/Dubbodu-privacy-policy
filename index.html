Dubbodu — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Developer: Team Navishka

Unofficial app. Dubbodu is an independent, student-led project by Team Navishka. It is not an official app of IISER Thiruvananthapuram and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the institute.
Accuracy disclaimer. Information displayed in the app (timetables, mess menus, bus timings, academic calendar, contacts, etc.) is aggregated on a best-effort basis and may not always be accurate or up to date. Use it at your own risk. Always verify with official institute sources for anything important.

Device permissions & local data (never collected)

The app uses certain on-device capabilities to function. This data is handled entirely on your phone and never leaves your device:

  • Location (GPS): Processed live on your device solely to display your position and calculate campus routes. We do not collect, log, or transmit your location.
  • Motion sensors: Gyroscope and orientation sensors are read live to rotate the 360° panorama viewer. Sensor data is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Timetable preferences: Saved strictly in your phone's local storage. Never uploaded.

What may be sent to servers

  • Standard server logs: When the app downloads campus maps, menus, or panoramas, our server may log standard technical request data (IP address, timestamp, app version) to keep the service running.
  • Device identifiers (planned for abuse prevention): To prevent scraping and abuse, the app plans to register an anonymous device identifier (Android ID + random install ID). This is not linked to your name, email, or any personal profile.
  • Institute email (tentative future possibility): There is a slight chance a future update might require logging in with an IISER TVM email address solely to restrict access to the IISER TVM community. If introduced, it will only be used for this access gate and nothing else. (This is prospective and may not happen.)

What we never do

  • Sell your data
  • Show ads or use ad networks
  • Track your location over time or build a profile on you
  • Store personal profiles or require accounts (aside from potential institute email verification solely for access restriction)

Third parties

  • Cloudflare — hosts and delivers app content (panoramas, data). May process your IP address as part of delivery.
  • Firebase (Google) — planned for anonymous device registration (abuse prevention only). No personal data involved.

Security

All network requests use HTTPS. The app's assets are encrypted. Cached panoramas on your device are stored encrypted and are only usable on your device. We apply reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

  • Location: Revoke in Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Permissions. Map and routing stop working; everything else still works.
  • Notifications: Revoke in Settings → Apps → Dubbodu → Notifications.
  • Uninstall: Removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Data requests: Contact us below.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest change.
